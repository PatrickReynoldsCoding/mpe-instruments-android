## understand the alternative music libraries



- TouchOSC and Lemur let people design and share touch controllers, even with scripting, but they make no sound. They only send MIDI or OSC.
- Decent Sampler, Kontakt and Pianobook-style libraries share instruments, but each comes with its own fixed way of being played.
- GeoShred and ThumbJam pair a great playing surface with great sounds, but you can't share or swap either part.

## The store and creator marketplace. Two problems:

- You'd need two groups of creators at once, instrument makers and controller makers. Very few people write Faust.

- Paid mobile instruments are a small market.
